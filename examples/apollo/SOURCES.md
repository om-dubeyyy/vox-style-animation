## Facts on screen

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Launched 16 July 1969 from Kennedy Space Center; the vehicle was 363 ft tall | NASA caption for S69-39961 |
| Erasable memory 2K (2,048) words; fixed memory 36K (36,864) words; 16-bit words; a one-megahertz clock; Block II weighed 70.1 lb (≈ 32 kg) | NASA, *Computers in Spaceflight*, ch. 2-5: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/computers/Ch2-5.html |
| 2,048 × 16-bit words ≈ 4 KB | arithmetic (2,048 × 2 bytes = 4,096 bytes) |
| Mission duration 8 days, 3 hours, 18 minutes; 16–24 July 1969 | NASA Apollo Lunar Surface Journal, Apollo 11 overview: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/alsj/a11/a11ov.html |
| Splashdown ~13°19′N 169°09′W, 24 July 1969 | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_11 (recovery section) |
| 8 GB ÷ 4 KB = 2,097,152 → "about two million times" | arithmetic; 8 GB is a typical phone, not a specific model |
