## People and principles (interviews and articles)

- **Joss Fong, Joe Posner, Phil Edwards, Sam Ellis, Estelle Caswell, Valerie Lapinski** (Vox): visual-first stories, "you can't bell and whistle your way out", archive before script, map verification, sound design as a pillar.
- **Joey Sendaydiego**, Vox video art director: "You don't want it to look perfect… more like an ad than an editorial piece"; one recurring visual idea per video.
- Sound: documentary mixing guides (music 18–24 dB under the voice) and whoosh-design guides (peak on the fastest frame; layer air, texture and landing).
- Motion: animation-on-twos references (12 drawings per second), line-boil settings (displacement 5–10 at 4–12 fps), and After Effects recreations of the look (paper on overlay ~75%, grunge ~35%, noise ~5%, vignette, edge-only colour fringing).

Every number in LOOK_BOOK.md is tagged by where it came from: measured (M), stated by a source (S) or practice (P). Tier A = Vox staff or the makers themselves; B = tutorials that recreate the look; C = secondary write-ups.
